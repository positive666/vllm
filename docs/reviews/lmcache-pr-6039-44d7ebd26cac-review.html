[Bugfix][MP] Drain stream callbacks before closing transfer contexts

Problem and result

Before

Closing the completion dispatcher before stream synchronization allows synchronization to publish callbacks after the final drain. Contexts can then be unavailable to their callbacks.

After

Synchronize registered streams while contexts and the dispatcher remain available. Stop and drain the dispatcher, then release contexts and preserve FD-channel cleanup. Synchronization errors propagate while resources remain retained.

This refresh retains the original close-ordering change and adapts the future import to its new upstream transport location.

Lifecycle and ownership

1. Stop submissions
2. Synchronize streams
3. Drain dispatcher
4. Release contexts and FD channel

The caller must stop new submissions before close. Transport wait=False alone does not establish this precondition. Hung device recovery and concurrent submission during close remain outside the fix.

问题与结果

修复前

若先关闭完成通知分发器，再同步设备 stream，同步期间产生的回调可能落在最终排空之后。回调也可能找不到仍需要访问的传输上下文。

修复后

保持上下文和分发器可用时同步已注册的 stream，再停止并排空分发器，最后释放上下文并保留 FD 通道清理。同步失败时抛出异常并保留资源。

此次同步保留原有 close 顺序修复，并把 future 导入适配到上游新的 transport 位置。

生命周期与所有权

1. 停止新提交
2. 同步 stream
3. 排空回调分发器
4. 释放上下文和 FD 通道

调用者须在 close 前停止新提交。单独设置 transport wait=False 无法保证这个前提。本修复不涵盖设备挂起恢复和关闭期间继续提交的情况。
