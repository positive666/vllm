Problem and result

Before

An aborted eager producer may already have submitted STORE. Immediate session retirement lets its late STORE recreate the session. An aborted request waiting for RETRIEVE has different completion ownership.

After

Extend final-STORE retirement to eligible eager aborted producers. Retain #6020 final-store ordering and #5938 receive-owned completion suppression. Loading and lazy paths retain their distinct lifecycle.

This refresh preserves runtime behavior from the existing PR and adapts renamed token/future imports. Retirement tests use independent copies of the current vLLM empty output instead of its removed constructor field.

Lifecycle and ownership

1. Abort after STORE
2. Check receive ownership
3. Wait for final STORE reports
4. Retire the session once

Depends on open #6020 and #5938. The dev-based diff includes those prerequisites and retains their authorship. Worker-loss recovery, RPC exceptions and unknown DMA completion remain outside this dependent draft.

问题与结果

修复前

被中断的 eager 生产者可能已经提交 STORE。立即退休会话会让迟到的 STORE 重建会话。等待 RETRIEVE 的中断请求具有不同的完成通知所有权。

修复后

把最终 STORE 回执屏障扩展至符合条件的 eager 中断生产者。保留 #6020 的最终写入顺序和 #5938 的接收方完成通知抑制。加载和 lazy 路径继续使用各自生命周期。

此次同步保留已有 PR 的运行行为，并适配迁移后的 token/future 导入。退休测试通过复制当前 vLLM 空输出构造独立对象，以兼容已移除的构造字段。

生命周期与所有权

1. STORE 后中断
2. 判断接收方所有权
3. 等待最终 STORE 回执
4. 仅退休会话一次
